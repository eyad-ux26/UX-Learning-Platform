# Videos and example products

Lesson videos and lesson steps often show an **example product**: a gym sign-up form, a banking app, a checkout page. If that example looks like UXGate, learners can't tell what is UXGate and what is the example. So every video and mock screen has two layers that never mix.

| Layer | What it is | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **UXGate layer** | Title card, captions, recap card, logo, pointers, selection frames, highlights, redlines, numbering, "before / after" labels | Always UXGate: night look, cyan highlights, Cairo and Titillium Web, Phosphor icons, UXGate illustrations |
| **Example layer** | The product being discussed: its screens, brand, content | Always the example's own industry style, never UXGate's |

## Rules for example products

1. **Give every example its own identity.** A fictional brand name, a simple logo mark, and a palette and type that fit its industry (see the starter table). The example should look like a believable real product in that field.
2. **Keep UXGate's signatures out of examples.** No UXGate logo or door face, no UXGate blue #005F99 or cyan #78E4FC as the example's main colour, no Cairo or Titillium Web, no ledge buttons, no UXGate 3D illustrations or Phosphor duotone art.
3. **Always frame the example.** Show it inside a phone, tablet, laptop or browser frame with neutral grey chrome, on a plain stage. Never let it fill the whole video frame as if it were the video's own interface.
4. **Label it.** On first appearance, put a small UXGate tag on the frame corner: "Example · Hana's Gym" / «مثال · نادي هناء». In lessons, mock screens keep their "MOCK" bar.
5. **Annotate only with the UXGate layer.** Pointers, the selection frame with handles, cyan highlights, numbered markers and redlines are always UXGate style and sit on top of the frame. That way learners always know which marks are our notes and which are the product.
6. **Before and after use the same example identity.** Only the design decision changes between the two, so the difference is the lesson, not the styling.
7. **One identity per recurring example.** If Hana's Gym appears in several clips and lessons, it keeps the same name, logo, colours and fonts everywhere. Keep each one as a short "example brand card" (name, industry, logo, 2 to 3 colours, fonts, tone) in a shared library.
8. **Fictional by default.** Use invented brands. Show a real product only when the lesson is about that product, with its name and source on screen, and never imitate a real brand closely enough to be mistaken for it.
9. **Its own theme.** An example can be light inside a night video, or dark in a light lesson. It follows its own product, not UXGate's theme.
10. **Readable on a phone.** On the 1080 canvas, example text is at least 28 px and the device frame takes at most about 70% of the height, so captions keep their 120 px bottom safe area.

## In the app too

The same split applies to mock screens inside lesson steps: the step frame, choices, Check bar and feedback are UXGate; the mock screen in the middle is the example product, in its own identity, inside a framed "MOCK" device.

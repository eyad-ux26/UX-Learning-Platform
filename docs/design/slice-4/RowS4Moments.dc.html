Rules kept

Quiet by default. Account, data, help, legal and system pages play no more than the usual tap and toggle, so a bigger sound always means something happened in learning. No blame sounds. 404, error, offline and a kept review never play error or wrong sounds. One meaning per sound. An accepted review reuses “also works”, a gold badge reuses badge-unlock. Emails are silent; the app plays the sound when the learner arrives. Illustrations (search, error, offline, key, mail, empty box, shield, support, feedback, article) all come from the UXGate illustration set already in the repo.

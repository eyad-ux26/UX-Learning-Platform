S3-12 · Loading between screens

Built in PR #155. Home is on screen and Paths was tapped. The tab lights up at once, a thin line in the logo gradient runs along the top (from the right in Arabic) and the old screen dims. After 400 ms the artboard loader fills the content area; the rail and the toolbar stay usable. Busy buttons keep their colour and add a spinner after the label.

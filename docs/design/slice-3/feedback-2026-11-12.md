## D1 · Skill check: change answers, move between questions, submit at the end (slide 2)

**Does this break a learning rule? No.** A skill check is a test that gives its feedback at the end, so reviewing before submitting is standard and fair.
- Research on changing answers (the "first instinct fallacy" studies) finds that learners who change an answer more often go from wrong to right than from right to wrong.
- Retrieval practice, which gives the learning benefit, has already happened the first time the learner answers.
- The small risk is that a later question cues the answer to an earlier one. Our check uses a new case per question, so the risk is low, and the check measures the skill, not memory of the wording.

Two rules keep it honest:
- Nothing is shown about right or wrong until the learner submits.
- After submitting, nothing can change. The unit-end retest of missed skills runs after the submit, as it does now.

Lessons are different. In lesson practice, the first answer still counts and the feedback is immediate. That doesn't change.

**Build** (LessonKinds, `mode=check`, `nav=mid|last`; board S3-19 frame 5):
- After an answer is saved, the bottom sheet shows the following:
  - "Answer saved" / «حُفظت إجابتك»
  - "You can change it until you submit. Results come at the end." / «يمكنك تغييرها حتى ترسل الاختبار. النتيجة في النهاية.»
  - A row of numbered question buttons, 40 px (44 px target). There are three states:
    - **answered:** solid border
    - **current:** selection colour plus ledge, `aria-current="step"`
    - **no answer yet:** dashed border
  - Each button's spoken name is "Question 2, answered".
- Choices stay tappable after the save, so tapping another choice replaces the saved answer and saves again. Confidence, if shown, is asked again for that question.
- Buttons:
  - **Back** / «السابق» (secondary): hidden on question 1.
  - **Save and next** / «احفظ وتابع» (primary).
  - On the last question, the primary button is **Submit the check** / «أرسل الاختبار» with a send icon.
- If any question has no answer on the last question, show the line "1 question has no answer yet. Tap a number to go back." / «سؤال واحد بلا إجابة بعد. اضغط رقمه للرجوع إليه.» Submit still works; an empty answer counts as wrong.
- Resuming after leaving returns to the first unanswered question, with saved answers kept.
- Engine: store per-question answers as drafts until submit, then score all at once. XP for the check is granted at submit.

## D3 · Revise any unit (slide 4)

Board S3-19 frame 4 (`PathsPages view=pathUnits`, `pathRevise`).
- On the path page, every unit the learner has opened (done or current) has a **Revise** / «راجع» button (secondary, small, review icon). Its spoken name is "Revise Unit 1 from the start". Locked units have none.
- Tapping it opens a sheet (a dialog on laptop):
  - Over line "Unit 1 · 4 lessons"; title "Revise this unit" / «راجع هذه الوحدة»; body "Go through the unit again to refresh what you learned." / «راجع دروس الوحدة من جديد لتثبّت ما تعلّمته.»
  - Three rows:
    - "All 4 lessons, from the start", with the videos, summaries and idea cards.
    - "Your skill results stay as they are": revising never lowers a skill, and the skill check is practice here.
    - "Replay XP": half XP, and the first replay today counts for the goal and streak. These are the same rules as the existing lesson replay (`xpFactor`).
  - Buttons: **Start revising** / «ابدأ المراجعة», and **Not now** / «ليس الآن».
- Revise mode:
  - It plays the unit's lessons in order from Lesson 1, with replay rules, and does not move the learner's "next lesson" pointer.
  - The lesson header shows a "Revising" tag.
  - Leaving and coming back resumes revise mode at the lesson it stopped on.
- The skill check at the end of a revised unit: **waiting on Eyad (card in the thread). The recommendation is to include it as practice**, with answers checked at the end and a result screen that says "Practice only. Your skill results didn't change". The other option is to end revise after the last lesson.

## D5 · Streak page easier to reach; tooltip on XP (slide 6)

Board S3-19 frame 2 (`PathsPages view=homeXpTip`).
- The streak counter in the header (phone and laptop) links to `/app/streak`. Its spoken name is "16 days in a row. Open your streak". The whole streak row in the Goal card also links there (D2 row 7).
- The XP counter becomes a button with a small info icon. Tapping it toggles a popover (300 px, under the counter, aligned to the end; it closes on Esc, on an outside tap, or on a second tap; `aria-expanded`). It contains:
  - Title "What XP is for" / «ما فائدة نقاط XP؟»
  - "XP shows how much you practised. It does not buy anything and never goes down." / «نقاط XP تُظهر مقدار تدرّبك. لا تشتري بها شيئًا ولا تنقص أبدًا.»
  - Three bullets:
    - "10 XP for a step you answer on your own, 5 with a hint."
    - "Replays give half XP."
    - "Weekly XP ranks you on the leaderboard, if you join it."
    - Arabic on the board.
  - Link "See how today's XP was counted" to the numbers page (`/app/streak` numbers section).
- The same tooltip goes on the XP counter in the path map header.

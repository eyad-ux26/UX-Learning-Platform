The UXGate tokens and type in a calmer work-tool layer. Same colours, fonts, radius and focus as the learner app; no rewards, no bounce, no decoration.

1 · Principles

Design-tool frameThe menu reads like a layers panel; the current page wears the selection frame with four handles. Detail opens in a right inspector.
Dense, not cramped14 px text, 13 px tables, 36 px controls, 42 px rows. One primary button per area.
Safe by defaultPersonal data is masked until revealed. Risky actions show their effects, ask for a reason and are logged.
English panel, Arabic contentThe panel is in English. Anything a learner reads is shown in Arabic and English, with Arabic right to left in Cairo.

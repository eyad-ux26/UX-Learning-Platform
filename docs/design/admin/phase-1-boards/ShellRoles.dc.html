How the menu works

  • Five groups in a fixed order: Overview, People, Money, Content, Grow, plus Admin for people who manage the team. A group with no allowed items is not shown.
  • The current page wears the UXGate selection frame with four corner handles, like a selected layer.
  • Counts are work waiting for this person; red means past a deadline.
  • Collapses to an icon rail on work pages with their own panes (support inbox) and below 1180 px; labels become tooltips.
  • People with several roles get the union of their menus.

Top bar, every role

Search with Ctrl K, the environment badge, the bell, help, and the person’s name and role. The same for every role; see it on any screen.

Search (Ctrl K) only returns what the role can open. The bell holds approvals, SLA alerts and system alerts. The environment badge is always visible.

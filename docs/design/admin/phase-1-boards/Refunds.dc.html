Refunds

Requests from learners and agents. The 7-day rule is checked for you; the refund itself happens at Paddle or Paymob.

Your role, Support agent, can approve refunds up to $50 (about 2,430 EGP) inside the 7-day window. Bigger refunds and exceptions go to a support lead, billing manager or admin for approval.
